Neuroscience × engineering

How do we learn and maintain dexteous skills? I am a scientist, engineer, and entrepreneur building tools and approaches for recording neural activity and tracking skilled behavior to understand physically intelligent systems. I lead a small, exeptionallly talented team at Morphosis Neurotech and act as a Project Lead at Lawrence Berkeley Lab / Cyclotron Road.
